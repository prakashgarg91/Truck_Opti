## 2. Free trial

- Every agency gets one 14-day free trial.
- No card is needed to start.
- The trial has Starter limits.
- One trial per agency, per GSTIN, and per mobile number.
- The trial does not convert to a paid plan automatically. You must choose and pay for a plan.
- When the trial ends with no paid plan, the agency moves to the expired state (section 4). Your data stays.

## 3. Billing

- Subscriptions are prepaid. You pay for a period before it starts.
- Renewal is manual. We do not charge your card again without your action. [OWNER: confirm no auto-debit at launch]
- Payments are taken through Razorpay, into the platform's own account.
- Every payment gets a tax invoice. GST is added only while the seller is registered under GST. At present the seller is not registered, so no GST is charged. Numbers look like TO/26-27/000001 and run in one unbroken series per financial year (April to March).
- Invoices show the platform's GSTIN and your GSTIN if you give it. Same-state supply shows CGST and SGST; other states show IGST.
- Invoices are never edited. A correction is made with a credit note (CN/26-27/000001).

## 4. Expiry, grace and renewal

- We send reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before the period ends, and on the end day.
- After the period ends, you get a 7-day grace period. In grace you can view and export everything, and update trip status. You cannot add trucks, drivers, staff or orders, and you cannot dispatch new jobs.
- After the grace period, the agency is expired. New dispatch stays locked.
- Your data is never deleted or hidden. You can always view and export it.
- To renew, open Subscription in the app, choose a plan, and pay. Access returns as soon as payment is confirmed.

## 5. Refunds

- If you ask within 7 days of your first purchase, we refund the unused part of that period, pro rata.
- After 7 days, or for any renewal, there are no refunds.
- The owner (platform admin) issues the refund through the payment provider. Money returns to the original payment method.
- A credit note is issued for the refunded amount.
- A refund ends the subscription at once. The agency moves to the expired state.

## 6. Cancellation

- You can cancel at any time by asking support by email.
- Cancellation stops renewal. There is no automatic renewal to cancel.
- Your paid access continues until the end of the paid period. We do not refund the rest of the period on cancellation, except under section 5.
- Cancelling does not delete your data.

## 7. Disputes, chargebacks and contact

- If you think a charge is wrong, contact support first. We aim to reply within [OWNER: days].
- If a payment is disputed with your bank (chargeback), the subscription may be suspended until the dispute is settled.
- Disputes that support cannot resolve go to the courts named in the governing law line above.

## 8. How freight money is handled

- TruckOpti does not hold, collect or move freight money.
- Each agency collects its freight from its clients into its own bank account.
- Payments on this page are only for TruckOpti subscriptions. They are not freight payments.
